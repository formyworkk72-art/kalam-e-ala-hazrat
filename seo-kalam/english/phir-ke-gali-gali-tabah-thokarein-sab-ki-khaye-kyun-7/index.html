PHIR KE GALI GALI TABAH THOKAREIN SAB KI KHAYE KYUN

Phir ke gali gali tabah thokarein sab ki khaye kyun
Dil ko jo aql de Khuda teri gali se jaye kyun

Rukhsat-e-qafila ka shor gash se hamein uthaye kyun
Sote hain unke saaye mein koi hamein jagaye kyun

Baar na the Habib ko paalte hi ghareeb ko
Royen jo ab naseeb ko chain kaho ganwaye kyun

Yaad-e-Huzoor ki qasam ghaflat-e-aish hai sitam
Khoob hain qaid-e-gham mein hum koi hamein chhudaye kyun

Dekh ke Hazrat-e-Ghani phail pade faqeer bhi
Chhayi hai ab to chhaoni hashr hi aana jaye kyun

Jaan hai Ishq-e-Mustafa roz fazo'n kare Khuda
Jisko ho dard ka maza naaz-e-dawa uthaye kyun

Hum to hain aap dil-figaar gham mein hansi hai nagawaar
Chhed ke gul ko naubahaar khoon hamein rulaye kyun

Ya to yun hi tadap ke jayen ya wohi daam se chhudayen
Minnat-e-ghair kyun uthayen koi taras jataye kyun

Unke jalal ka asar dil se lagaye hai qamar
Jo ke ho loot zakhm par daagh-e-jigar mitaye kyun

Khush rahe gul se andaleeb khaar-e-haram mujhe naseeb
Meri bala bhi zikr par phool ke khaar khaye kyun

Gard-e-malaal agar dhule dil ki kali agar khile
Barq se aankh kyun jale rone pe muskuraye kyun

Jaan-e-safar naseeb ko kisne kaha maze se so
Khatka agar sahar ka ho shaam se maut aaye kyun

Ab to na rok aye Ghani aadat-e-sag bigad gayi
Mere Kareem pehle hi luqma tar khilaye kyun

Raah-e-Nabi mein kya kami farsh-e-bayaz deeda ki
Chadar-e-zill hai malgaji zer-e-qadam bichhaye kyun

Sang-e-dar-e-Huzoor se hum ko Khuda na sabr de
Jaana hai sar ko ja chuke dil ko qarar aaye kyun

Hai to Raza nira sitam jurm pe gar lajaye hum
Koi bajaye soz-e-gham saaz-e-tarab bajaye kyun
