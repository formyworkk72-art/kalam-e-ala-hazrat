PUL SE UTARO RAH GUZAR KO KHABAR NA HO — Tazmeen 1

Tazmeen (Owais Raza Qadri)

Daman mein yoon chhupa lo ke digar ko khabar na ho
Hoor-o-malak ko, jinn-o-bashar ko khabar na ho
Jannat mein aise pahunchu, Saqar ko khabar na ho
Pul se utaaro, raah guzar ko khabar na ho
Jibreel par bichhaein to par ko khabar na ho

༺❁━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━❁༻

Bujh jaaye ab kahin na charagh intezaar ka
Juz tere koi aur nahi dil-figaar ka
Jaaye khazaan mein dekh loon ab munh bahaar ka
Kaanta mere jigar se gham-e-rozgaar ka
Yoon kheench leejiye ke jigar ko khabar na ho

༺❁━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━❁༻

Ummat se pyaar tera na aaye shumaar mein
Rakkha na ek pal bhi kabhi intezaar mein
Bechain qalb aaya usi pal qaraar mein
Faryaad ummati jo kare haal-e-zaar mein
Mumkin nahi ke khair-e-bashar ko khabar na ho

༺❁━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━❁༻

Dar-dar ki thokaron se, Khudaya bacha hamein
Sadqa Hasan-Husain ka kar de ata hamein
Duniya ke ranj-o-gham se tu kar de riha hamein
Aisa gumaa de un ki wila mein Khuda hamein
Dhoonda kare par apni khabar ko khabar na ho

༺❁━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━❁༻

Jin ka maqam karte bayaan hon Shah-e-Zamaa'n
Kaise phir un ki shaan bayaan kar sake zabaa'n
Khud Mustafa ne Maula Ali par kya ayaa'n
Farmate hain ye dono hain sardar-e-do-jaha'n
Ae Murtaza! Ateeq-o-Umar ko khabar na ho

༺❁━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━❁༻

Ye ishq hi to hai jo sabaq hum ko padhaye
Ye ishq hi to hai jo hamein raaz ye sikhaye
Hadd-e-adab ye ishq sitam jitne dil par khaye
Ye ishq ka usool hai, nikle na lab se haaye
Ae Khaar-e-Taiba! dekh ke daaman na bheeg jaaye
Yoon dil mein aa ke deedah-e-tar ko khabar na ho

༺❁━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━❁༻

Imaan hai ke koi Khuda se bada nahi
Mahboob hain Khuda ke woh lekin Khuda nahi
Hukm-e-shara mein sajda bhi un ko kiya nahi
Ae shauq-e-dil! ye sajda gar un ko rawa nahi
Achha! woh sajda kijiye ke sar ko khabar na ho

༺❁━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━❁༻

Ahbaab, Ubaid! jaan chhidakte hain jo yahan
Honge ba-roz-e-hashr kisi ke bhi woh kahan
Maan-baap kaam aayein na aulaad hi wahan
Un ke siwa, Raza! koi haami nahi jahan
Guzra kare pisar pe pidar ko khabar na ho
