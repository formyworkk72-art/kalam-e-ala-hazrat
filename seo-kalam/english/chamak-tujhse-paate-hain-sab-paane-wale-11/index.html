CHAMAK TUJHSE PAATE HAIN SAB PAANE WALE

Chamak Tujh Se Paate Hain Sab Paane Wale
Mera Dil Bhi Chamka De Chamkaane Wale

Barasta Nahi Dekh Kar Abr-e-Rahmat
Bado'n Par Bhi Barsa De Barsaane Wale

Madine Ke Khitte Khuda Tujh Ko Rakhe
Ghareebo'n Faqeero'n Ke Thehraane Wale

Tu Zinda Hai Wallah Tu Zinda Hai Wallah
Meri Chashm-e-Aalam Se Chhup Jaane Wale

Main Mujrim Hoon Aaqa Mujhe Saath Le Lo
Ke Raste Mein Hain Ja Baja Thaane Wale

Haram Ki Zameen Aur Qadam Rakh Ke Chalna
Are Sar Ka Mauqa Hai O Jaane Wale

Chal Uth Jab'ha-Farsa Ho Saqi Ke Dar Par
Dar-e-Jood Ae Mere Mastaane Wale

Tera Khaaye Tere Ghulamon Se Uljhein
Hain Munkir Ajab Khaane Ghurrane Wale

Rahega Yun Hi Unka Charcha Rahega
Pade Khaak Ho Jaayein Jal Jaane Wale

Ab Aayi Shafa'at Ki Saa'at Ab Aayi
Zara Chain Le Mere Ghabraane Wale

Raza Nafs Dushman Hai Dam Mein Na Aana
Kahaan Tum Ne Dekhe Hain Chand Raane Wale
