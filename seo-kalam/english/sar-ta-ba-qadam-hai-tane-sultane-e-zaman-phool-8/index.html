SAR TA BA QADAM HAI TANE SULTANE-E-ZAMAN PHOOL

Sar ta ba qadam hai tan Sultan-e-Zaman phool
Lab phool dahan phool zaqan phool badan phool

Sadqe mein tere baagh to kya laye hain ''ban'' phool
Is ghuncha-e-dil ko bhi to eema ho ke ban phool

Tinka bhi hamare to hilaye nahi hilta
Tum chaho to ho jaye abhi koh-e-mehan phool

Wallah jo mil jaye mere gul ka paseena
Maange na kabhi itr na phir chahe dulhan phool

Dil basta-o-khoon gashta na khushboo na latafat
Kyun ghuncha kahoon hai mere Aaqa ka dahan phool

Shab yaad thi kin daanton ki shabnam ke dam-e-subah
Shokhaan-e-bahari ke jadaau hain kiran phool

Dandan-o-lab-o-zulf-o-rukh-e-shah ke fidaai
Hain durr-e-Adan, la'al-e-Yaman, mushk-e-Khutan phool

Boo ho ke nihaa'n ho gaye taab-e-rukh-e-shah mein
LO ban gaye hain ab to haseenon ka dahan phool

Hoon baar-e-gunah se na khajil dosh-e-azeezan
Allah meri na'ash kar aye jaan-e-chaman phool

Dil apna bhi shaidaai hai us nakhun-e-pa ka
Itna bhi mah-e-nau pe na aye charkh-e-kuhan! phool

Dil khol ke khoon ro le gham-e-aariz-e-shah mein
Nikle to kahin hasrat-e-kho'n-naabe-shudan phool

Kya ghaza mala gird-e-Madina ka jo hai aaj
Nikhre hue jo ban mein qayamat ki phaban phool

Garmi ye qayamat hai ke kaante hain zaba'n par
Bulbul ko bhi aye saaqi-e-sahba-o-laban phool

Hai kaun ke gir ye kare ya fatiha ko aaye
Bekas ke uthaye teri rehmat ke bharan phool

Dil gham tujhe ghere hain Khuda tujhko woh chamkaye
Suraj tere khirman ko bane teri kiran phool

Kya baat Raza us chamanistan-e-karam ki
Zehra hai kali jis mein Husain aur Hasan phool
