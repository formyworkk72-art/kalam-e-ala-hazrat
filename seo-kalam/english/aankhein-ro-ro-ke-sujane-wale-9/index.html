AANKHEIN RO RO KE SUJANE WALE

Aankhein Ro Ro Ke Sujane Wale
Jaane Wale Nahi Aane Wale

Koi Din Mein Yeh Sara Ojad Hai
Are O Chhauni Chhaane Wale

Zabah Hote Hain Watan Se Bichhre
Des Kyun Gaate Hain Gaane Wale

Are Bad-Faal Buri Hoti Hai
Des Ka Jangla Sunane Wale

Sun Lein Aada Mein Bigadne Ka Nahi
Woh Salamat Hain Banane Wale

Aankhein Kuch Kehti Hain Tujh Se Paigham
O Dar-e-Yaar Ke Jaane Wale

Phir Na Karwat Li Madine Ki Taraf
Are Chal Jhoote Bahaane Wale

Nafs Mein Khaak Hua To Na Mita
Hai Meri Jaan Ke Khaane Wale

Jeete Kya Dekh Ke Hain Ae Hooro!
Taiba Se Khuld Mein Aane Wale

Neem Jalwe Mein Do Aalam Gulzaar
Waah Waah Rang Jamaane Wale

Husn Tera Sa Na Dekha Na Suna
Kehte Hain Agle Zamaane Wale

Wohi Dhoom Unki Hai Masha Allah
Mit Gaye Aap Mitaane Wale

Lab-e-Sairaab Ka Sadqa Paani
Ae Lagi Dil Ki Bujhaane Wale

Saath Le Lo Mujhe Main Mujrim Hoon
Raah Mein Parte Hain Thaane Wale

Ho Gaya Dhak Se Kaleja Mera
Haaye Rukhsat Ki Sunaane Wale

Khalq To Kya Ke Hain Khaaliq Ko Azeez
Kuch Ajab Bhaate Hain Bhaane Wale

Kushta-e-Dasht-e-Haram Jannat Ki
Khirkiyan Apne Sirhaane Wale

Kyun Raza Aaj Gali Sooni Hai
Uth Mere Dhoom Machaane Wale
